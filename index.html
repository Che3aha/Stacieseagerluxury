Meet Stacie Seager

Based in Scottsdale, Arizona, Stacie Seager is a Master Hair Stylist specializing in Custom Luxury Extensions, Signature Blonding, and Bespoke Color. Certified in multiple premier extension lines and techniques, Stacie combines her mastery of each discipline to create breathtaking, one-of-a-kind transformations that blend seamlessly.

Stacie’s passion for life-changing hair began in childhood and evolved into a passionate devotion to the art of transformation—creating hair that allows a woman to step into the version of herself she’s always imagined. From restoring lush, natural-looking hair for clients experiencing medical hair loss to creating exquisite transformations that make the seemingly impossible possible, Stacie provides the gateway for women to finally embody the hair they’ve always dreamed of—and all the magic it sets in motion. ✨

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before your hair extension consultation and transformation.

How long does the full transformation take?
From consultation to installation, your transformation is completed in about one week.

This allows time for custom color or blonding if needed and precise matching of your premium extensions.
Will extensions damage my natural hair?
I'm certified in multiple extension methods, which allows me to customize your install based on your hair's texture, density, and strength. If needed, I'll use different methods in different areas of your scalp to make sure everything looks natural and feels comfortable.
How long have you been doing this?
I have 22+ years of experience and I am certified in multiple brands and methods, allowing me to use the best technique or combination of techniques based on your hair type, density, and strength.
How long do hair extensions last?
Move-ups are done every 6–8 weeks to keep your extensions comfortable, maintain your natural hair health, and preserve the integrity of the hair.
What makes your color matching different?
I focus on creating a completely seamless blend. During your consultation, I carefully analyze your natural color, undertones, and desired look to custom match your extensions so there are no harsh lines or mismatched tones.
Do you work with all hair types?
Yes. I work with all hair types and textures. During your consultation, I'll recommend the best method specifically for your hair to ensure the most natural and long-lasting result.
What's included in the consultation?
Your consultation includes a full hair assessment, color analysis, goal planning, method recommendation, and a personalized quote. There's no pressure; it's simply to see if we're the right fit for each other.
